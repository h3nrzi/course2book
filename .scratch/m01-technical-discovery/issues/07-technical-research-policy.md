## Question

How should the production workflow obtain and retain sufficient primary-source evidence for technical claims and separately labeled «بیشتر بدانید» enrichment?

Determine research triggers, source/version selection, the relationship between course-era behavior and current documentation, and the evidence a citation must retain for inspection and later rechecking. Address unavailable documentation, unverifiable material claims, conflicting sources, and avoiding silent replacement of the instructor's teaching. Distinguish factual checking of the source-derived lesson from adding externally sourced teaching.

Investigate the available documentation tools before comparing workflows. Respect the repository's Context7-first rule for library/API documentation and verify material claims against the source that owns them.

Resolve evidence and uncertainty policy and the responsibility boundary; defer citation syntax and tool-specific prompts where safe. Do not select publishing or reader link handling.
