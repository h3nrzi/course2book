# Choose intermediate artifact authority and data flow

Type: grilling
Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Mode: HITL
Blocked by: 03, 08
Parent: [Technical Discovery for M01 — Content Production](../map.md)
Requirements: P07, P15, P18, P20, P23–P24, P26; C3, C5

## Question

What is the smallest set of inspectable intermediate artifacts and authority rules that carries source evidence, analysis, authored text, research, examples, and review information between independently runnable responsibilities?

Compare human-editable documents, structured companion records, and combined approaches. Decide what is authoritative versus derived, how artifacts identify the source or content revision they describe, and how humans can correct an intermediate outcome without creating conflicting sources of truth. Preserve the private/public boundary and the distinctions established for source-derived teaching and enrichment.

Resolve semantic contracts, ownership, minimum format choices that genuinely block specification, and the conceptual data flow. Do not design every field or filename, introduce a general workflow database by assumption, or decide the approval and recovery policies that later questions own.

## Dependency rationale

- [Choose the private material and public repository boundary](03-private-material-boundary.md): The privacy boundary constrains artifact authority and location.
- [Choose independent code example generation and verification](08-independent-code-examples.md): Example verification establishes the additional content and evidence that handoffs must carry.

Source preparation, authorship, and research contracts are prerequisites through example verification and must also inform this answer.
