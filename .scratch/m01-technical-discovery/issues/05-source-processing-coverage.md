# Choose source preparation and instructional coverage traceability

Type: grilling
Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Mode: HITL
Blocked by: 04
Parent: [Technical Discovery for M01 — Content Production](../map.md)
Requirements: P03, P05–P07, P09; C1–C3; AC-M01-02–AC-M01-03; PRD §13

## Question

How will original transcript evidence survive preparation and analysis so a human can trace substantive teaching into a drafted lesson and see uncertainty or omissions?

Decide the relationship between preserved input, cleaned working text, instructional points, source locations, and later coverage evidence. Address corrections that might change technical meaning, missing visual references, qualifications and prerequisites, and lessons too large for one practical context. Determine how processing can be divided and reconciled without silently dropping teaching or attributing invented material to the instructor.

Compare lightweight traceability with finer-grained tracking using representative examples. Resolve the coverage and source-preservation contract and the architectural handling of long lessons; defer exact segmentation algorithms, thresholds, identifiers, and serialization to later work where safe.

## Dependency rationale

- [Choose transcript intake, lesson identity, and course ordering](04-transcript-intake-identity.md): The intake identity and supported input contract determine what source evidence can be preserved and located.
