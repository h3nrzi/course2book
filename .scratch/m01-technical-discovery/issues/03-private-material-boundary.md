# Choose the private material and public repository boundary

Type: grilling
Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Mode: HITL
Blocked by: None
Parent: [Technical Discovery for M01 — Content Production](../map.md)
Requirements: P03, P25–P26; C8; PRD §13; M01 side of PUB07

## Question

Where should private transcripts, derived content, intermediate artifacts, review evidence, and execution records live relative to the public repository, and what prevents accidental public exposure?

Compare the simplest feasible separation approaches, including a private workspace outside the public checkout and an explicitly excluded local content area. Investigate actual repository tracking and ignore behavior before recommending an approach. Address how repository-local skills locate private material, preserve source access, and avoid leaking excerpts or personal paths through public discovery records, research assets, logs, or fixtures.

Resolve the ownership and protection boundary and the required safeguards, with alternatives and trade-offs. Exact private directory names, backup products, hosting, access control for the reader, and publishing transport are outside this decision. Do not move real source material as part of the decision.
