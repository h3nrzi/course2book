# Choose the approved M01 output boundary with M02

Type: grilling
Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Mode: HITL
Blocked by: 13
Parent: [Technical Discovery for M01 — Content Production](../map.md)
Requirements: P21–P23; C3–C6, C8; M01 side of PUB01–PUB02, PUB04–PUB05, PUB07

## Question

What minimum approved content and evidence must M01 make identifiable to a future publisher, and how is that boundary protected from drafts, stale approvals, and private production material?

Resolve the semantic handoff for course/book and original lesson identity, ordering, title and authored body, examples, «بیشتر بدانید», references, and the exact approved revision. Determine what revision or content identity information M01 must preserve so M02 can later distinguish approved updates and support downstream continuity, without choosing a reading-position model.

Compare a minimal immutable approved view with other simple handoffs. Establish what M01 owns and what remains for M02. Do not design publication packages, distribution, publishing triggers, hosting, reader storage, sync, or position relocation. Any need to choose those would cross this map's destination.

## Dependency rationale

- [Choose review, focused revision, and explicit approval semantics](13-review-revision-approval.md): The handoff cannot identify an Approved Lesson revision until approval binding and invalidation semantics are settled.
