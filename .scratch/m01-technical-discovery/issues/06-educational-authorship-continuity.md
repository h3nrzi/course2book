# Choose educational authorship and approved course context

Type: grilling

Requirements: P08–P13; C1–C2, C4–C5; AC-M01-04, AC-M01-08

## Question

What authoring contract turns analyzed source teaching into expanded Persian educational prose while preserving fidelity and continuity with earlier Approved Lessons?

Determine how the author distinguishes source-derived teaching, explanatory elaboration, independent examples, external enrichment, and unresolved source limitations. Establish how useful pedagogical structure and predominantly English technical vocabulary are guided and evaluated without requiring a rigid chapter template or artificial length target.

Choose how approved terminology and necessary prior course context become available to later lessons without rewriting earlier approvals or loading the entire course. Compare minimal direct use of approved context with derived continuity aids, including their authority and freshness implications.

Resolve responsibility and context boundaries. Do not write a production prompt, style guide, complete sample chapter, or new product requirement during this decision.

## Dependency rationale

- [Choose source preparation and instructional coverage traceability](05-source-processing-coverage.md): Authorship must use the agreed source-point and uncertainty contract rather than inventing its own fidelity model.
